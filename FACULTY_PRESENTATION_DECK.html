The Engineering Problem

Why Existing Smart Refrigerators Fail

Commercial solutions (Samsung Family Hub, LG InstaView) cost ₹1.5L – ₹3L and suffer from fatal blindspots.

1. The Opaque Container Blindspot

Optical cameras only see surface packaging. They detect a milk carton, but have zero physical capability to tell if it contains 1000 mL, 200 mL, or is empty.

2. Piezoresistive Cold Drift

At 4°C, load cells experience metallic thermal drift of 50g to 80g over hours, falsely triggering phantom consumption alerts on single scales.

3. Tropical Optical Condensation

Warm humid room air condenses instantly on cold camera lenses upon door opening, blurring all captured frames without active intervention.

4. Multi-Liquid Ambiguity

A single scale cannot mathematically isolate simultaneous usage across two bottles: $x + y = \Delta W$ has infinite mathematical solutions.

End-to-End Operation

System Architecture & Data Flow

STEP 1

Door Opens

Reed switch separates. Interrupt fires on ESP32. Immediately latches baseline tare weights: $W_{0,\text{dairy}}$ and $W_{0,\text{drinks}}$.

STEP 2

Door Closes

Reed switch closes. System enforces a strict 1.2s damping delay to allow mechanical vibration and liquid sloshing to settle.

STEP 3

Flash & Dual Sample

Flash LED pulses in the dark. Camera snaps frame ($I_1$). Dual HX711s sample settled masses ($W_{1,\text{dairy}}, W_{1,\text{drinks}}$).

STEP 4

Laptop Inference & UI

Laptop runs YOLOv8n, computes remaining milliliters, updates SQLite, and pushes live updates to the web dashboard and Flutter mobile app in real time!

Wi-Fi / Hotspot Telemetry: JSON Payload + Multipart JPEG Total Latency: < 1.8 seconds post-closure
Intellectual Property & Novelty

The 4 Patentable Innovations

Crafted to strictly overcome Section 3(f) [Mere Aggregation] and Section 3(k) [Software per se] of the Indian Patents Act, 1970.

1. Dual-Zone Split Cantilever Claim 1 & 6

Two independent cantilever plates separated by a 2mm air gap. Eliminates multi-liquid ambiguity ($x + y = \Delta W$) with 100% mathematical determinism at ₹160 cost.

2. Event-Gated Tare Latch Claim 2

Latching $W_0$ at door opening and subtracting it from $W_1$ post-closure cancels long-term piezoresistive thermal drift across the brief 10-second access window: $\Delta W = W_1 - W_0$.

3. Passive Parasitic Anti-Fogging Claim 3

Standby thermal dissipation (0.26W) from the ESP32 processor is heatsunk to the optical aperture window, keeping it above the dew-point with zero active heating elements!

4. Opaque Liquid Quantification Claim 4 & 5

Couples bounding-box visual persistence with load cell mass delta ($\Delta W < 0$) to compute remaining fluid volume without requiring internal immersion probes.
