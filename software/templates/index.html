ChillSense Retrofit Technology

Anti-Fog Optical Baffle: The custom 3D-printed enclosure mounts magnetically to the interior ceiling of any conventional refrigerator. A 65° conical baffle and physical flash isolation ring completely eliminate condensation and glare.

Multi-Sensor Fusion: Combines dual 24-bit HX711 ADCs with high-precision cantilever load cells, a DHT22 environmental sensor, and an MC-38 magnetic door switch. When you close the door, an overhead 100ms flash triggers and delivers real-time inference with zero food disturbance.
