Context & Motivation

Introduction

The Food Waste Crisis

Domestic households discard 15–20% of edible groceries annually due to expiration and lack of visibility into opaque containers (milk, juices, condiments).

Cost & Adoption Barrier

Commercial smart refrigerators (Samsung Family Hub, LG InstaView) cost ₹1,50,000 to ₹3,00,000, completely locking out 95% of existing refrigerator owners.

The Retrofit Paradigm

A non-invasive plug-and-play module for existing refrigerators priced under ₹3,000 INR, democratizing food inventory management and reducing spoilage.

Core Proposition: Upgrade any standard single or double-door domestic refrigerator without drilling, wiring cuts, or voiding manufacturer warranty.

Unsolved Engineering Problems

Challenges & Research Gap

1. The "Opaque Container" Blindspot

Computer vision models can recognize a milk bottle or juice carton bounding box, but cannot optical penetrate opaque HDPE plastic or tetra-paks to measure internal volume.

2. Piezoresistive "Cold Drift" at 4°C

Conventional strain-gauge load cells drift significantly (50–80g) over hours in cold 4°C refrigerated air due to thermal contraction of aluminum beams and gauge adhesives.

3. Camera Lens Dew-Point Fogging

When the refrigerator door opens in tropical/humid ambient air, warm moisture instantly condenses on cold glass optics, rendering vision models blind.

4. The Multi-Liquid Ambiguity Problem

A single load cell shelf cannot mathematically isolate simultaneous liquid usage ($x + y = \Delta W$). If a user pours both milk and juice, a single scale cannot determine individual drops.

👉 Our Research Gap: A unified multi-sensor fusion architecture that decouples mass sensing across dedicated zones while canceling thermal drift and optical fogging.
Targets & Deliverables

Project Objectives

Obj 1

Low-Cost Non-Invasive Retrofit Form Factor

Design a self-contained, battery/USB powered smart insert and magnetic camera pod installable in < 5 minutes on any refrigerator shelf for under ₹3,000 INR.

Obj 2

Differential Dual-Zone Gravimetric Sensing

Implement independent dual-cantilever load cell channels with an event-gated tare latch that cancels 4°C thermal drift and isolates multi-liquid usage with ±2g precision.

Obj 3

Passive Anti-Fog Optical Camera Pod

Engineer a 3D-printed enclosure with a 65° conical flared baffle, optical isolation collar, and parasitic microcontroller heat harvesting to eliminate lens fogging with 0W extra power.

Obj 4

Real-Time Mobile & Cloud Inventory Ecosystem

Develop a local/cloud REST backend (Flask + SQLite) paired with a responsive Web Dashboard and Flutter mobile app with animated liquid gauges and automated grocery checklists.

Methodology & Design

Proposed Methodology & System Architecture

Event-Gated Multi-Sensor Fusion Pipeline
Phase 1: Access Event

Door opens ➔ Reed switch trips ➔ ESP32 latches baseline tare weights $(W_{0\text{a}}, W_{0\text{b}})$.

Phase 2: Settling & Strobe

Door closes ➔ 1.2s slosh dampening delay ➔ 100ms flash strobe ➔ ESP32-CAM captures frame.

Phase 3: Mass Fusion

Dual HX711s sample settled masses $(W_{1\text{a}}, W_{1\text{b}})$. $\Delta W$ isolates liquid consumption cleanly.

Phase 4: Sync & UI

JSON HTTP POST ➔ Flask backend ➔ YOLOv8 fallback ➔ Live Web Dashboard & Flutter Mobile App.

Hardware Hub ESP32 + ESP32-CAM (OV2640/3660), 2x 5kg load cells, 2x HX711, DHT22, MC-38 reed switch.
CAD & Enclosure Parametric OpenSCAD 2-part pod, 65° conical baffle, internal flash isolation collar, PETG print.
Software & App Python Flask 3.0 REST API, SQLite DB, YOLOv8 vision pipeline, Flutter cross-platform companion app.
